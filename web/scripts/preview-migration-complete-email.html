Why Skillset exists

Your prompts are IP. AI is everywhere. We protect them.

As AI models keep multiplying, prompts and skills are getting copied, screenshotted, and quietly absorbed into training data. The most valuable thing you build is also the easiest to lift.

We built Skillset to flip that. Your prompts and skills are encrypted at rest and in transit, executed inside the app, and shared on your terms — not exposed to the model provider in raw form.

✓ Your account moved with you

Your sign-in, prompt packs, credits, and history are all preserved in the same cloud account. Sign in at skillset.so with your existing email and you're back where you left off.

Inside the app

Skill Chat

Chat with every model from one window. No more juggling Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini in separate tabs.

Skill Flow

Orchestrate multi-step skills in one call. Plans, executes, and merges — without you babysitting it.

Skill Router

Routes each prompt to the model that's actually best for it — speed, cost, or reasoning depth.

Skill Preset MVP

Encrypted, packaged skills you can run without revealing the underlying prompt. Built to stop AI theft at the source.

Skill Control

Per-skill model, credit, and access controls. Decide who runs what, with which model, and at what cost.

Native installers

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Windows (x64 & ARM64) — signed, sandboxed, auto-updating.

Sunsetting extensions

We're shutting down all browser extensions (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) and the MCP server. Browser surfaces couldn't enforce the encryption guarantees we wanted, and they pulled focus from the place skills actually run.

Everything those did — and a lot more — now lives inside the Skillset app. One surface, one fast path, fewer moving parts to break.
